A contract is a stack of clauses, and every clause has a type a reviewer must recognise.

Governing Laws   <- This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
Notices          <- All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent by certified mail.
Counterparts     <- This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.

GPT-2 is a generator, not a classifier, so each example becomes ONE string for it to learn:

  This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. <Governing Laws>
  All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent by certified mail. <Notices>
  This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original. <Counterparts>

At test time we give it only the clause, and the label token it generates is the prediction.
